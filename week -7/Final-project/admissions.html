Admissions

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements
  • Minimum 60% marks in Intermediate (Pre-Engineering)
  • Valid score in MUET Entry Test
  • Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry in Intermediate
  • No third division in Matric/Intermediate
Other Requirements
  • Age limit: Maximum 23 years
  • Pakistani citizenship
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Character certificate

Important Dates

Event Date Details
Application Submission January 1 - March 31, 2024 Online application through MUET portal

Test Result Announcement May 1, 2024 Available on MUET website
