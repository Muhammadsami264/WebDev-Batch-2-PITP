About MUET

Our History

Mehran University of Engineering & Technology (MUET) was established in 1976 as a public research university located in Jamshoro, Sindh, Pakistan. Over the years, MUET has evolved into one of Pakistan's premier engineering institutions, known for its commitment to academic excellence and innovation.

The university has played a pivotal role in shaping the engineering landscape of Pakistan, producing graduates who have made significant contributions to industry, academia, and society.

Mission

To provide quality education in engineering and technology, foster research and innovation, and contribute to the socio-economic development of Pakistan through the production of skilled professionals and cutting-edge research.

Vision

To be a world-class center of excellence in engineering education, research, and innovation, producing leaders who drive technological advancement and societal progress in Pakistan and beyond.

Leadership Message

Message from the Vice Chancellor

"At MUET, we are committed to nurturing the next generation of engineers and innovators. Our focus is on providing a holistic education that combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills, preparing our students to tackle real-world challenges and contribute meaningfully to society."
